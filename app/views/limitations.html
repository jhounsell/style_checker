Style checker limitations

The style checker can be used to assess if content follows the Home Office style guide consistently. It cannot tell you if your content is fit for purpose and meets user needs.



Terms not covered by the Style Checker
Term Reason
British nationality Entry is more around guidance than prescribing a style point
British nationality (overseas) Investigating. Needed to distinguish between usage of the visa term and the national, from context.
Deportation This is guidance on how to use the term, less a style prescription.
Gender This entry just links to the sex and gender pattern.
Ministers Investigating. This entry just links to the GDS style guide.
Naturalisation This is guidance on how to use the term, less a style prescription.
Passport control Investigating.
Quotes and speech marks This entry just links out to the GDS style guide.
Sex This is guidance on how to use the term, less a style prescription.

If a term does not appear here, then it is included in the style checker rules.
