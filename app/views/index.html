Home Office style checker

This tool checks if your content follows Home Office style guide rules. It is designed to help with content quality assurance and analysis.

What the checker will check

The checker will flag:

  • words or abbreviations to avoid
  • where capitalisation is incorrect on specific terms
  • if a specific term should be 1 word instead of 2 (or vice versa)
  • if acronyms are not explained on the first mention (where they should be)

What the checker will not check

The checker will not flag:

  • general capitalisation issues - only on specific terms
  • syntax or grammatical errors
  • incorrect American spellings
  • puncutation, spacing and formatting issues
  • readability or structural problems

The style checker has limitations

It cannot tell you whether your content is fit for purpose and meets user needs. Read more about its limitations.
